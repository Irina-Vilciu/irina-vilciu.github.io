About Irina Vilciu

Lecturer at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, POLITEHNICA Bucharest, in the Department of Measurements, Electrical Apparatus and Static Converters.

A multidisciplinary engineering background.

Irina Vilciu’s academic work spans electrical measurements and instrumentation, environmental monitoring, and materials recovery and recycling.

She completed a doctorate in Materials Science and Engineering in 2007 and has pursued doctoral studies in Electrical Engineering since 2023, focusing on environmental monitoring with UAVs after fires.
